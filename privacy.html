Valt Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Valt is an independent app built and operated by one person. This policy describes what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: support@thevalt.app.

What we collect

Account information. Your email address, a username, a display name, your date of birth, and an optional bio. Your password is never stored. We store only an argon2id hash of it, from which the password cannot be recovered.

Date of birth. Creating an account requires your full date of birth, which we ask for to confirm you are at least 13. It is stored with your account as a date only, with no time, so that we can confirm the account holder's age if it is ever in question, for example if someone reports that an account belongs to a child. It is not used for anything else. It is kept for as long as your account exists, and is deleted with your account. It is never shown to other users, and the app never displays it, including to you. Only we can read it, directly in our database. Accounts created before we began asking for a date of birth hold none, and are not asked for one.

Content you create. Your reviews, ratings, play status, spoiler flags, comments, the accounts you follow, the games you mark as favourites, and the artwork you choose for your profile and reviews. We also store when each of these was created and last edited.

Profile photo. A profile photo is optional. You choose it with the system photo picker, which runs outside the app. Valt receives only the photo you pick, gets no access to your photo library, and asks for no permission. Before your photo is checked or stored, it is cropped to a square, resized, and stripped of all its metadata, including any location. Every photo is then checked automatically by Amazon Rekognition before anyone can see it, and a photo the check refuses is not stored. When you replace or remove your photo, or delete your account, it is deleted from our storage within about an hour. Copies already shown on a device, or held by Amazon CloudFront, which delivers photos, can last up to a day.

Reports. When you report a review, a comment, a person, or a profile photo, we keep the report so we can review it: who reported it, the reason, and any details you write. A report is deleted when we dismiss it, when the reported review or comment is deleted, or when the account that posted the reported content or the account that reported it is deleted. When a report is made, we are normally sent an email about it. It contains the reporter's username and internal id, the reason, any details they wrote, and who or what was reported, with an excerpt of a reported review or comment. We keep that email in our support inbox as a moderation record, including after the report itself is deleted.

Reported photos. When someone reports a profile photo, we keep a private copy of it that only we can see, so the report can be reviewed even if the photo is changed or removed. The copy is kept for as long as the report exists: until we remove the report, or until the account that posted the photo or the account that reported it is deleted. It is deleted within about two hours after that. If a photo appears to show the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child, we report it to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, as the law requires. We keep a copy of it and the details of the account for the period the law requires, even if the account is deleted.

Technical information. Your IP address is held briefly in server memory to limit repeated signup, login, and password reset attempts. It is kept for up to thirty minutes for login and password reset, and up to two hours for registration, is never written to a database or a file, and is discarded when the server restarts. Our server logs record when an error occurs and which part of the service it occurred in. They also record when a password reset or email verification code is sent to your account or held back, when a report is received, and, for profile photos, whether an upload was accepted or refused and why, with the names of the automatic check's categories and its version. These entries may include your account's internal id, an identifier that is not your username. Scheduled maintenance tasks record only counts. Server logs never record your email, password, IP address, or the photo itself.

Password resets. If you ask to reset your password, we store a keyed hash of the one time code we email you, when it was issued, and a count of recent requests, alongside your account. The code itself is never stored. It expires after fifteen minutes, and the record is deleted once the code is used, when your account is deleted, or within about a day of expiring.

Email verification. When you create an account, we email you a one time code to confirm your address, and you can ask for a new code from within the app. When we send a code, we store a keyed hash of it, when it was issued, when it expires, how many times it has been tried, and a count of codes sent recently, alongside your account. The code itself is never stored. It expires after thirty minutes, and the record is deleted once the code is used, when your account is deleted, or within about a day of expiring. Once you confirm your address, we store the date and time you confirmed it with your account, for as long as your account exists. We email your account address only to send a verification or reset code, or to confirm that your password was changed.

What we do not collect

Valt contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. There are no third party software kits in the app. We do not collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any device or advertising identifier, and the app has no access to your photo library. The only photo we receive is a profile photo you choose. The app requests no system permissions.

What is public

Your username, display name, profile photo, bio, reviews, ratings, comments, follows, and favourite games are visible to anyone using Valt. Your profile photo can also be opened by anyone who has its web address. Your email address and date of birth are never shown to other users.

Third parties

Game information and artwork come from IGDB, owned by Twitch. When you search for a game, the text you type is sent to IGDB to run the search. Game cover and background images are loaded by your device directly from IGDB's image servers, which means IGDB receives your device's IP address for those requests. IGDB's own handling of that data is governed by Twitch's privacy notice.

Valt runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States. AWS stores and processes the data described above on our behalf.

Profile photos are stored in Amazon S3 and delivered to devices through Amazon CloudFront. When a device loads a profile photo, CloudFront receives that device's IP address, as with any web request. Amazon Rekognition checks each photo as described above, and we have opted out of AWS using this content to improve its services.

Email is sent through Amazon Simple Email Service. If a message to you cannot be delivered, or you mark one as spam, Amazon reports your address to our support inbox so we can stop sending to it.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or transfer it to data brokers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your account, your profile photo, your reviews, your comments, your follows in both directions, your lists, and your artwork choices. Your profile photo is deleted from our storage within about an hour, and any private copy of it kept for a report within about two hours, except where the law requires us to keep it, as described above. Because comments belong to the review they sit under, comments other people left on your reviews are removed along with them. Deleted accounts cannot be recovered. Database backups are retained for 7 days and then expire.

If you do not confirm your email address within twenty four hours of creating your account, the account is deleted automatically and without notice. Asking for a new code does not extend this. The account is deleted in the same way as if you had deleted it yourself: everything listed above is removed, and it cannot be recovered. An account whose address has been confirmed is never deleted this way.

Signing out of the app clears your saved session and any cached content on the device.

Security

Passwords are hashed with argon2id. All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Your session token is stored in the iOS keychain, marked so that it is not synced to iCloud or included in encrypted backups.

Children

Valt is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. When you create an account we ask for your date of birth. If it shows you are under 13, no account is created, and nothing you entered is stored, logged, or emailed. If you believe a child has an account anyway, contact us at support@thevalt.app and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app. For anything else, contact support@thevalt.app.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Continuing to use Valt after a change means you accept the updated policy.
