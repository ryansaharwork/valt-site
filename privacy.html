Valt Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Valt is an independent app built and operated by one person. This policy describes what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: support@thevalt.app.

What we collect

Account information. Your email address, a username, a display name, and an optional bio. Your password is never stored. We store only an argon2id hash of it, from which the password cannot be recovered.

Content you create. Your reviews, ratings, play status, spoiler flags, comments, the accounts you follow, the games you mark as favourites, and the artwork you choose for your profile and reviews. We also store when each of these was created and last edited.

Technical information. Your IP address is held briefly in server memory to limit repeated signup, login, and password reset attempts. It is kept for up to thirty minutes for login and password reset, and up to two hours for registration, is never written to a database or a file, and is discarded when the server restarts. Server error logs may record that an error occurred but do not record your email, password, or IP address.

Password resets. If you ask to reset your password, we store a keyed hash of the one time code we email you, when it was issued, and a count of recent requests, alongside your account. The code itself is never stored. It expires after fifteen minutes, and the record is deleted once the code is used, when your account is deleted, or within about a day of expiring.

Email verification. When you create an account, we email you a one time code to confirm your address, and you can ask for a new code from within the app. When we send a code, we store a keyed hash of it, when it was issued, when it expires, how many times it has been tried, and a count of codes sent recently, alongside your account. The code itself is never stored. It expires after thirty minutes, and the record is deleted once the code is used, when your account is deleted, or within about a day of expiring. Once you confirm your address, we store the date and time you confirmed it with your account, for as long as your account exists. We email your account address only to send a verification or reset code, or to confirm that your password was changed.

What we do not collect

Valt contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. There are no third party software kits in the app. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any device or advertising identifier. The app requests no system permissions.

What is public

Your username, display name, bio, reviews, ratings, comments, follows, and favourite games are visible to anyone using Valt. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Third parties

Game information and artwork come from IGDB, owned by Twitch. When you search for a game, the text you type is sent to IGDB to run the search. Game cover and background images are loaded by your device directly from IGDB's image servers, which means IGDB receives your device's IP address for those requests. IGDB's own handling of that data is governed by Twitch's privacy notice.

Valt runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States. AWS stores and processes the data described above on our behalf.

Email is sent through Amazon Simple Email Service. If a message to you cannot be delivered, or you mark one as spam, Amazon reports your address to our support inbox so we can stop sending to it.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or transfer it to data brokers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your account, your reviews, your comments, your follows in both directions, your lists, and your artwork choices. Because comments belong to the review they sit under, comments other people left on your reviews are removed along with them. Deleted accounts cannot be recovered. Database backups are retained for 7 days and then expire.

If you do not confirm your email address within twenty four hours of creating your account, the account is deleted automatically and without notice. Asking for a new code does not extend this. The account is deleted in the same way as if you had deleted it yourself: everything listed above is removed, and it cannot be recovered. An account whose address has been confirmed is never deleted this way.

Signing out of the app clears your saved session and any cached content on the device.

Security

Passwords are hashed with argon2id. All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Your session token is stored in the iOS keychain, marked so that it is not synced to iCloud or included in encrypted backups.

Children

Valt is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at support@thevalt.app and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app. For anything else, contact support@thevalt.app.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Continuing to use Valt after a change means you accept the updated policy.
